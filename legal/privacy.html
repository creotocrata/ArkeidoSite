Arkeido Privacy Policy

Applies to: Arkeido Cyclops, Arkeido Link and the ArkEngine share service
Version: 1.0 · Effective date: 12 November 2026

Short version

  • Your models are rendered on your computer. They are not sent to us.
  • The Software sends us no telemetry, no usage statistics and no crash reports.
  • To check a paid licence, it sends the licence key and an anonymous computer code to our store.
  • If you publish a presentation link, the presentation files are stored on our share service until the link expires (plus 30 days), then deleted.

1. What data, why, and on what legal basis

1.1 Purchases (Polar)

When you buy, Polar (Polar Software, Inc., USA) collects your name, email, billing address, payment details and IP address. Polar is the Merchant of Record and acts as an **independent controller** under its own privacy policy (https://polar.sh/legal/privacy). We receive your name, email, country, order and licence details to deliver the licence and give you support. Basis: contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)); legal obligations for invoices and tax (art. 6(1)(c)).

1.2 Licence activation and checks

When you activate a licence, and then about once a day when online, the Software sends to the Polar licence-key API:

  • the licence key;
  • an instance code: a short hash computed on your computer from the computer name, the user name and the platform. The names themselves are never sent and cannot be read back from the code;
  • the major SketchUp version (e.g. "SketchUp 2023"), shown next to the code in your list of activations.

Polar returns whether the key is valid, for which product, and how many computers use it. *Basis: contract (art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in preventing licence misuse (art. 6(1)(f)).*

1.3 Data kept only on your computer

The Software stores on your computer, and does not send: your settings, your licences (%APPDATA%\Arkeido\licenses.dat on Windows), the trial start date, render history, logs and caches. Uninstalling does not delete them; you can delete those folders yourself.

1.4 Blender download

If you let the Software download Blender, it downloads the official build directly from download.blender.org. The Blender Foundation then sees your IP address, as with any download. We receive nothing.

1.5 Share service (ArkEngine presentation links)

Only when you publish a presentation (Cyclops Pro and Plus):

  • What is stored: the presentation files (3D geometry, textures, lighting data, the viewer), the title, the expiry date, the size, and — if you set one — the password as a salted hash (we cannot read it). The owner is recorded as a hash of your licence key, not as your name or email.
  • Who can see it: anyone with the link, or with the link and the password if you set one.
  • How long: until the expiry you choose (7–365 days), plus 30 days so you can renew it, then it is deleted automatically. You can delete it earlier.
  • Viewers of the link: we use no analytics and no tracking. A password-protected link sets one strictly necessary cookie (ark_<id>) so the viewer does not ask for the password on every file; it expires automatically. The viewer saves the chosen quality in the browser's local storage. Cloudflare processes the viewer's IP address to deliver the page and protect the service.

The presentations may contain information about your clients or projects. **For that content, you are the controller and Arkeido is your processor:** we store it only to serve the link, follow your instructions (publish, renew, delete), use sub-processors listed in §3, and help you answer data subject requests. Basis: contract (art. 6(1)(b)).

1.6 Support emails

If you write to us, we use your email and message to answer. Kept for up to 3 years after the last contact. Basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)).

2. What we do not do

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, do not profile you and do not make automated decisions with legal effects. The Software does not scan or upload your models.

4. How long we keep data

  • Orders and invoices: as required by Portuguese tax law (currently 10 years).
  • Licence records: while the licence is valid and up to 2 years after it ends.
  • Shared presentations: see §1.5.
  • Support emails: see §1.6.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to geral@arkeido.com. We answer within one month.

You may also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the **Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)**, www.cnpd.pt, or to the authority where you live.

6. Security

Connections to the store and the share service use HTTPS. Licence files are sealed so they cannot be edited or copied to another computer. Share-service passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

7. Children

The Software is meant for professionals and students aged 16 or over.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version with a new date and, for important changes, tell you in the Software or by email.
